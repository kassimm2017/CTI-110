# How to Make a Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich for a Robot

## Assumptions
1. The robot has no prior knowledge of what a sandwich is
2. The robot takes every instruction literally and needs exact details
3. All ingredients are on the table: loaf of bread, peanut butter jar, jelly jar, plate, butter knife
4. The robot has two arms with hands

## Steps
1. Walk to the table that has the ingredients on it
2. Pick up the plate with your left hand and place it in the center of the table
3. Use your right hand to pick up the loaf of bread
4. Open the loaf and remove two slices of bread with your left hand
5. Lay both slices flat on the plate side by side
6. Pick up the peanut butter jar with your left hand
7. Twist the lid counter-clockwise with your right hand to open it
8. Pick up the butter knife with your right hand
9. Dip the knife into the peanut butter jar and scoop out 1 tablespoon of peanut butter
10. Spread the peanut butter evenly onto one slice of bread using the knife
11. Place the knife down on the plate
12. Pick up the jelly jar with your left hand
13. Twist the lid counter-clockwise with your right hand to open it
14. Pick up the knife again with your right hand
15. Dip the knife into the jelly jar and scoop out 1 tablespoon of jelly
16. Spread the jelly evenly onto the second slice of bread
17. Place the knife down on the plate
18. Pick up the second slice (with jelly) and flip it over so jelly faces down
19. Place it on top of the first slice (with peanut butter) to form a sandwich
20. Close both jars by twisting lids clockwise and put everything back

## Done When
The sandwich is complete with two slices of bread, peanut butter and jelly on the inside, sitting in the center of the plate, and both jars are closed with the knife on the plate.
